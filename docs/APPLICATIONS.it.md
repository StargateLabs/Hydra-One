## Additional official application identities / Altre identità ufficiali

The native workspace also contains original application resources for **Antigravity** and **Claude Desktop**. Their role is native workspace/host integration, not an inference-engine implementation. Publisher assets for Qwen, DeepSeek, Google, Mistral, OpenBMB, HuggingFaceTB, Meta and Spark identify catalogue publishers/models only; they are not an assertion of universal compatibility.
